A NOTE FROM BABODY

To the apple of my eyes,

Here is Badbad... Here is Badbad, the person who loved you, who was loved by you, and who still loves you to this day, more deeply than words could ever express,even more than he has ever loved himself. Here is Badbad, the one whose place for you in his heart has never changed. Neither time, nor distance, nor circumstances could ever take that place away from you. Here is Badbad, who loved you blindly, like someone walking through endless darkness, unable to see what lies ahead, yet still holding on to the path because he knows exactly where his heart wants to go. Here is the person who made mistakes, more than once, and regretted them even more. The one who fears being the reason behind your sadness, or hurting you, even in the smallest ways, including those you might not even notice. The one who cannot bear the thought of your pain being connected to him, or of the very hands that once longed to hold you becoming the reason for your wounds. The truth is, I haven't been okay. I haven't truly been myself for quite some time. So many burdens and problems have gathered upon my shoulders, weighing me down as though they were more than one person could possibly carry. Sometimes, I feel as if I am fighting endless battles, exhausting every part of myself just trying to stand again, while no one truly knows the extent of what is happening inside me. And what hurts me the most is seeing you drift away from me because I have fallen short in the way I treat you, because of something I did without realizing it, or because of words I failed to choose carefully. I hate myself when I realize that I have become the reason for your sadness. God knows, there is nothing more painful to me than seeing you troubled or wounded because of me. Love was never meant to make me a source of your pain. All I ever wanted was to be the place where you could find comfort, peace, and safety. I am sorry for changing during my baccalaureate years, for no longer being the person you had grown accustomed to. I never wanted you to carry the weight of my mental struggles, nor did I want to pull you into the exhaustion I was going through. In my own misguided way, I was trying to face everything alone, believing that distancing myself would protect you from my pain. I never realized that my silence and the walls I built around myself could hurt you too. I also apologize for my lack of emotional maturity, for every moment I failed to understand your feelings or express my own in the way you deserved. I am truly, deeply sorry. And even though I saw you choose someone other than me at one point in our story, I tried to respect your choice because I knew I had played a part in what had happened. I had fallen short in so many ways, especially when I needed to reach out for help, yet I chose to shut myself away instead of extending my hand to you. Still, that did not make the pain any less real. I was shattered when I saw you with someone else. It hurt far more than I was ever able to admit. When you came back to me, I had not completely healed. A part of me returned to you, but some of that sadness remained buried deep within my heart, like a wound that had never fully closed. I was afraid that one day someone else would come along and take you away from me, leaving me once again facing a loss I was never ready for. With time, I slowly began to heal. I tried to make peace with what had happened, to move beyond those feelings, and to mend what the past had left broken inside me. I never wanted yesterday's wounds to control our relationship today, nor did I want to make you carry the weight of a pain for which you were not solely responsible. And all of this was happening while I was fighting my own battles, facing endless problems, and trying to hold myself together through days that were never easy and nights when even sleep could not quiet my mind or ease the weight of everything I was carrying. And despite it all... My love for you has never changed. I may have changed in some of my actions. I may have failed to express myself, and there may have been times when I could no longer be the person you wanted by your side. But your place in my heart has never changed. I am not writing this to justify my mistakes, nor to make you feel guilty for the pain I have endured. I am writing because I want you to understand that behind the person whose changes you may never have fully understood, there was someone fighting a war within himself, while simultaneously fearing the loss of the one person he never wanted to lose. Here is Badbad... with all his mistakes, regrets, weaknesses, and feelings that he did not always know how to express. Here is the person who may not have been perfect in the way he loved you, but who never stopped seeing you as someone extraordinary in his life. And if there is only one thing I want you to know, it is this: Everything about me may have changed, but none of it was ever enough to change one simple truth. You still mean the world to me.

With a love that time could never change ♡
